# Decision log

The project owner approves decisions. This log records the direction the sketch is built from. Working title: Luxury Living, Within Reach.

| Date | Decision | Status | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 Oct 2026 | The concept is fictional and public-facing. Homes are not for sale. | Confirmed | A portfolio and audience-feedback concept, not a brokerage. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | UK-wide idea, shown through one notional worked example. | Confirmed | Avoids implying one price fits every part of the UK. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Three home roles: Considered Starting Point, Signature, Elevated. | Confirmed | A range, with the same information on each. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Equal emphasis on beautiful homes and financial clarity. | Confirmed | The monthly picture sits inside the home story. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Visual direction is Monograph combined with Plan and Ledger. | Provisional | Architecture-first frames, serif headlines, sans interface, integrated cost panels. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Purchase, mortgage, and energy figures in the sketch are design prototypes. | Provisional | Round numbers with assumptions printed beside them. Not valuations or offers. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Consumer-facing brand name. | Open | The working title stays until a naming decision. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Logo, final palette, commissioned imagery. | Open | The sketch uses provisional tokens and empty image frames. |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Virtual brand personality and social posts. | Open | Left out of this sketch so the page can be understood without a host. |
