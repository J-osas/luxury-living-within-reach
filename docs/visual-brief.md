# Visual brief

Status: provisional. Working title only. No logo, final name, or finished palette is chosen here. Reviewed 21 October 2026.

## Direction

A hybrid of two territories:

- Monograph: quiet, editorial, architecture first. Large type, generous margins, image frames treated like plates in a book.
- Plan and Ledger: the cost of a home is a designed part of the home story. Figures use a visible grid, tabular numbers, and an annotation colour.

The homepage gives equal emphasis to the homes and to financial clarity. Cost panels sit inside the home story.

## Type

- Headlines: Fraunces, a high-contrast serif, via `next/font`.
- Interface, labels, and body: Source Sans 3.
- Figures: the serif, with tabular numbers.

## Colour

Provisional tokens in `app/globals.css`:

- Ground `#f3efe6`, a warm off-white.
- Ink `#1c1915` for text. Soft ink `#3f3a34`. Muted `#4a453f`.
- Annotation `#6e2f24`, an iron oxide. Used for labels, rules on the cost panel, and figure emphasis.
- The annotation colour is not a sustainability theme. Green, leaves, and solar icons are not part of the identity.

Body text and annotation text on the ground both clear WCAG AA.

## Imagery

Frames are empty plates labelled “Concept visualisation. Architecture image to come.” This sketch does not use stock interiors or generated photographs of people or houses.

## Interaction

A short fade on the page, removed when the visitor prefers reduced motion. Focus is a 2px outline. Controls are at least 44px on the short side. The key-facts column sticks on wide screens and stacks on narrow ones, so it does not cover the story.
